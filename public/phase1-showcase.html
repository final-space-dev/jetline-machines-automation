Motion & animation tokens

JetlineUI 2.0 uses three timing tokens: --t-fast .14s for micro-interactions, --t-base .24s for transitions, --t-slow .4s for reveals. Two easing curves: --ease (natural) and --ease-out (spring-like). Hover each card to preview.
